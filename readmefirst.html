Get Organised (Before You Send Anything)

Spending ten minutes setting up a tidy system before you send your first request saves hours of searching later:

  1. Create one main folder on your computer: Name it CHC Claim, and create a separate subfolder inside it for each organisation you contact (for example: GP Surgery, Care Home, Local Council, Hospital Trust, and ICB).
  2. Prepare a ready-to-attach verification pack: Scan or photograph clear copies of your Proof of Identity (passport or driving licence), Proof of Address (utility bill or bank statement dated within the last 3 months), and your Legal Authority (Registered Lasting Power of Attorney, Deputyship Order, Grant of Probate, or signed Letter of Authority) so you can attach all three to every request in seconds.
  3. Create a matching folder in your email account: Set up a CHC Claim label or folder in Gmail or Outlook and keep every email chain with each provider in a single thread so you have a dated paper trail if you need to chase or escalate.
  4. Log every request immediately: Open the Correspondence Tracker as soon as you press send, enter the date, and export your statutory deadline alarms to your calendar.
Where to Find Email Addresses

Use the directories and tips below to find the right contact email for each organisation:

GP Surgeries

Check the practice website under Contact Us or Privacy Notice for the Practice Manager or Data Protection Officer email, or search the NHS Find a GP directory on nhs.uk. If only a general reception address is listed, mark the subject line for the attention of the Practice Manager.

Care Homes & Care Agencies

Look up the provider on the Care Quality Commission directory at cqc.org.uk or check the care home's own website. Send record requests to the Registered Manager and fee ledger requests to the head office Accounts or Finance team.

Integrated Care Boards (ICBs)

Search nhs.uk for your local Integrated Care Board, then open the Contact Us, Freedom of Information / Subject Access, or Continuing Healthcare page on the ICB website to find their Data Protection Officer and CHC team email addresses.

Local Councils (Social Services)

Find your council via gov.uk/find-local-council. Search the council website for Subject Access Request or Data Protection Officer for care assessments, and Adult Social Care Finance for fee ledgers and financial assessments.

NHS Hospital & Mental Health Trusts

Find the trust on nhs.uk and check its website for Access to Health Records, Medical Records, or Subject Access Requests. Most NHS trusts have a dedicated medical records disclosure email address.

Hospices & Specialist Clinics

Search the hospice directory at hospiceuk.org or check the clinic's website under Privacy Policy or Patient Information for their Information Governance or Caldicott Guardian contact email.

Tip if an organisation only has a telephone number or web form: Call reception and ask for the email address of the Data Protection Officer or Access to Health Records team so you have a written, timestamped record of your request. Always specify that you require full, unredacted records for an NHS Continuing Healthcare / retrospective funding claim.
