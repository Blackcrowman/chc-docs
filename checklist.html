1. Authority to Act
Establish your legal authority to access records and manage the claim.
Requirement Got It?
Authority To Act: (Need 1 of these 4)
Signed Letter of Authority from Claimant (if living, where they have capacity)
Power of Attorney Finance or Health (if living)
Will & Proof Of Executorship (if deceased)
Letter of Authority from Estate (if deceased) if not Executor
2. Absolute Minimum
The vital information providers and what's needed.
Requirement Got It?
GP Practice Manager
Full medical records, summary care records, historical consultations & GP notes.
Care Home Manager / Care Agency
Care plans, daily logs, eMAR, falls diaries, risk assessments, Waterlow/MUST & cost statements.
Important: Keep Scrolling for Sections 3 & 4
GP and care home records are the minimum needed to start, but the more supporting evidence you gather from the extra providers below (such as district nursing, hospital discharge summaries, and social care files), the stronger your claim will be. Check Sections 3 and 4 before sending your requests.

3. Additional Care Providers
GP and care home records are the minimum needed to start, though the more supporting evidence you can gather from these providers, the stronger your claim will be.
Requirement Got It?
Local Authority / Social Services
Social care assessments, financial contribution records, social work notes & safeguarding files.
Community Health / District Nursing
District nursing notes, tissue viability/wound assessments, occupational therapy & continence notes.
Integrated Care Board (ICB)
Previous DST assessments, MDT meeting notes & formal checklist refusal documentation.
Hospital Inpatient & Outpatient Services
Discharge summaries, inpatient notes, A&E attendances & specialist outpatient clinic letters.
4. Specialist Care Providers & Additional Evidence
Additional specialist clinical records and benefit award notices (add if applicable).
Requirement Got It?
NHS Mental Health Trust
MHA detention docs, psychiatric assessments, CPA reviews, Section 117 plans & tribunal records.
NHS Memory Clinic / Older Adult Services
Cognitive assessments, ACE/MMSE sheets, diagnosis records & consultant letters.
NHS Community Falls Prevention Service
Falls & balance assessments, bone health screening & environmental safety reports.
Hospice / Palliative Care Provider
Inpatient notes, palliative MDT reviews, end-of-life pathways & symptom control charts.
Community Dental / Special Care Dental
Dental health assessments, domiciliary visit notes & records of oral pain or infection.
Optometry / Ophthalmology Providers
Sight test records, visual acuity assessments & specialist ophthalmology clinic notes.
Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards (DoLS) Records
DoLS authorisation forms, best interests & mental capacity assessments.
DWP Attendance Allowance & Carer's Allowance Records
Benefit award notices, care component assessments, and historical correspondence.
