NHS CHC & PUPoC Claim Checklist
1. The Basics
Gather your core details and get ready to kick things off.
Requirement Got It?
Full Name for PAF (Person Advocated For)
Date of Birth for PAF
NHS Number for PAF
Address(es) & Postcode(s) for PAF for period of claim
Name of Advocate (You)
Claim Start and End Dates (can be decided later)
GP Name & Surgery Address
Authority To Act: (Need 1 of these 3)
• Power of Attorney Finance or Health (if living)
• Will & Proof Of Executorship (if deceased)
• Letter of Authority from Estate if not Executor
2. Care & Medical Records (Essential)
Use our Template Pack to get these quickly and easily.
Requirement Got It?
Medical Records Full notes
Care Plans Detailed (including Falls Diaries & Risk Assessments)
Daily Care Logs and Notes Detailed (including Waterlow & MUST scores)
eMAR (Electronic Medication Administration Records)
Incident and Accident Reports (including A&E & Ambulance 999 records)
Care Provider Cost Statements (for claim period)
3. Supporting Evidence
These aren't necessary but useful if available.
Requirement Got It?
DST Records, MDT Meeting Notes & Refusal Documentation
Hospital Discharge Summaries & Letters
Memory Clinic notes
Care Notes Prior to Care Home Admission
Local Authority Social Care Records
Specialist Clinical Assessments (TVN, OT, Continence)
DoLS Records
Mental Health Act Sectioning Records (e.g., Section 2 or 3)
DWP Attendance Allowance Records & Award Notices
DWP Carer's Allowance Letters & Records
