2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data required to operate our website and deliver our digital products:

  • Intake Waitlist Enquiries (Carrd): If you join a waitlist for our Audit, Claim, or Appeal services, we collect your email address and the service category you selected.
  • Digital Toolkit Purchases (Payhip & Stripe): When you purchase the £49 Self-Help Template Pack, Payhip and Stripe collect your name, email address, billing address/country (for fraud and accounting verification), payment confirmation, and order number. Payment card details are processed exclusively by Stripe and are never seen or stored by CROWDROP LTD / CHC Blueprint.
  • Direct Emails: If you email info@chcblueprint.co.uk, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling using your personal data (though Stripe performs automated fraud screening during card checkout).

3. Client-Side Interactive Tools (Zero Medical Data Collected)

Our free NHS CHC Claim Checklist (checklist.html) and toolkit Correspondence Tracker (tracker.html) run entirely inside your own web browser. Any claimant names, dates of birth, NHS numbers, addresses, or correspondence dates you enter are saved locally on your own device (via browser localStorage) and are never transmitted to, viewed by, or stored on our servers.

Do not email sensitive health data: Please do not send medical records, care logs, or special-category health data to us while Phase 2 client intakes are closed.

4. Lawful Bases for Processing (UK GDPR Article 6)
  • Contract Performance (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing your name, email address, and order details to deliver your digital toolkit, send your receipt and contract confirmation, provide technical support, and verify your £49 Audit Credit.
  • Consent / Pre-Contractual Enquiry (Article 6(1)(a) & (b)): Processing your email address when you ask to be notified as soon as client intake slots open.
  • Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): Retaining financial transaction records for statutory UK tax and HMRC accounting compliance.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use advertising, behavioural tracking, or third-party marketing cookies on chcblueprint.co.uk or our GitHub-hosted guides. Strictly necessary technical cookies may be set by our hosting and checkout providers (Carrd, Payhip, and Stripe) solely to ensure page security, form submission, and payment processing function properly. As noted in Section 3, our interactive checklist and tracker use your browser's local storage solely on your own device.

7. Data Security & Retention

We protect personal data using HTTPS/TLS encryption, multi-factor authentication on business accounts, and strict data minimisation.

  • Purchase & Tax Records: Order records (name, email, order number, amount paid) are retained for 6 years in accordance with HMRC accounting requirements and to honour your £49 Audit Credit.
  • Intake Waitlist Emails: Retained only until we notify you of an upcoming intake slot or until you ask us to remove your email address, whichever is earlier.
  • General Enquiries: Routine email correspondence is deleted once resolved, usually within 12 months.
8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to request:

  • Access to a copy of the personal data we hold about you (Subject Access Request).
  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"), subject to our statutory HMRC tax-retention obligations.
  • Restriction of processing or to object to processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability for data processed automatically under contract or consent.
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time (for example, leaving an intake waitlist).

To exercise any of these rights, email info@chcblueprint.co.uk. We respond to all valid requests within one calendar month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
